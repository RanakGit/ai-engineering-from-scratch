## Adding a lesson

The repository guide in `AGENTS.md` says a new lesson should live under
`phases/NN-phase-slug/MM-new-lesson/`. Add its `docs/en.md`, implementation,
tests, and `quiz.json`; optionally include an output artifact. Then link it in
`README.md`, update its status in `ROADMAP.md`, validate it, and commit using a
message such as `feat(phase-NN/MM): add <slug>`.
